About me

Medicine is where curiosity meets responsibility. Here is how I approach the path.

From the first dissection hall to late-night revision for university exams, MBBS has shaped how I think: systematically, patiently, and always with the patient at the centre. In my second year, I am deepening my grasp of how the human body works at every level—so that clinical years feel like a natural next step, not a leap.

I value clear communication, ethical practice, and lifelong learning. Outside structured coursework, I enjoy staying current with medical literature, peer discussions, and activities that keep empathy and teamwork sharp.
